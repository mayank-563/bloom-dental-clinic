Common questions

Before you book

Do you accept new patients? +

Yes — most new patients are seen within a week, and emergency slots are available same-day regardless of whether you've visited before.

Do you take dental insurance? +

We work with most major PPO plans and offer an in-house membership plan for patients without insurance. Bring your card to your first visit and we'll check your coverage.

How long does an Invisalign consultation take? +

About 30 minutes — including a digital scan and a same-visit preview of your projected results before you commit to anything.

What if I'm nervous about visiting the dentist? +

Tell us when you book. We offer paced appointments, sedation options, and staff specifically experienced with dental anxiety.

Our story

Started by one dentist tired of waiting rooms that feel like hospitals.

Bloom opened in 2008 with a simple idea: dental care doesn't have to feel cold, rushed, or intimidating. Dr. Elena Marsh started the practice out of a single chair — today it's grown to a full team, without losing the parts that made patients stay.

We still keep appointments unrushed. We still explain everything before we do it. And we still believe the best dental visit is the one you don't dread going to.
